# Employee Absenteeism Power BI Dashboard
This project presents a comprehensive Power BI dashboard analyzing employee absenteeism using two joined datasets: Absenteeism Data and Employee Info Data, connected via a common ID field.

The dashboard is designed to provide HR teams, data analysts, and decision-makers with actionable insights into patterns of employee absenteeism, health-related risk factors, and trends across time and demographics.

## 🧩 Data Sources and Integration
Absenteeism Table: Contains data on absence reasons, absence time in hours, monthly distribution, disciplinary actions, workload averages, and seasons.

## Employee Info Table: Includes demographic and health information such as age, BMI, smoking/drinking status, distance to work, health status, and education level.

These tables were joined on the ID column, which uniquely identifies each employee, allowing for the enrichment of absenteeism data with employee-specific attributes.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Normal Weight Employees Had the Highest Absenteeism: Contrary to expectations, employees classified under the normal BMI range recorded the highest total absenteeism time, suggesting absenteeism may be driven by other factors such as job stress or seasonal illness rather than just weight-related health issues.

- Spring Was the Season with the Most Absences: Analysis by season revealed that spring had the highest concentration of absentee hours. This could be linked to seasonal allergies, flu cases, or workload patterns during that period.

- Medical Reasons Were the Leading Cause of Absenteeism: Absences due to medical appointments and health-related issues dominated all other categories, highlighting a critical area for HR wellness programs or health policy reviews.

- March Recorded the Highest Absenteeism: A monthly breakdown showed that March had the highest total absenteeism, possibly aligning with seasonal health issues or annual workload peaks.

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- KPI Cards: Displaying total absenteeism hours, disciplinary failures, average workload, and absenteeism rates.

- Interactive Filters: By season, reason for absence, health status, smoking/drinking habits
